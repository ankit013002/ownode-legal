Ownode

Your AI. On your hardware. Anywhere.

Ownode connects your iPhone to AI software running on a computer you already own. Your prompts never reach us in readable form. They are encrypted end-to-end between your phone and your own computer.

Common questions

  • Do I need an account? No. Ownode has no accounts. You pair your computer by scanning a code, and that is the whole setup.
  • Does Ownode work without internet? Yes. On your own Wi-Fi, your phone talks to your computer directly and nothing passes through our servers.
  • Can you read my conversations? No. They are encrypted between your phone and your computer, and we hold no key that could decrypt them.
  • Where is my history stored? Only on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes it.
